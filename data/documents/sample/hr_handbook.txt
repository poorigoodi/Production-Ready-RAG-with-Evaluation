Acme Employee Handbook
Human Resources — Confidential internal document

Working Hours
Standard working hours are 09:00 to 17:30 local time, Monday to Friday. Employees may use flexible start times between 08:00 and 10:00 with manager approval.

Paid Time Off
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid vacation per calendar year. Vacation requests should be submitted at least 10 business days in advance. Unused vacation may carry over up to 5 days into the next year.

Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 15:00 local time when remote. Company-issued laptops must use full-disk encryption.

Parental Leave
Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks of paid parental leave. Leave must begin within 12 months of the child's birth or adoption.

Expense Policy
Business travel requires pre-approval for trips exceeding 300 USD. Meal expenses are reimbursed up to 60 USD per day. Personal entertainment is not reimbursable.
