Acme Product FAQ — NovaBook Laptop Series

Battery Life
The NovaBook 14 provides up to 12 hours of mixed-use battery life. Battery health diagnostics are available in the system settings under Power.

Ports and Connectivity
NovaBook 14 includes two USB-C Thunderbolt ports, one HDMI 2.1 port, and a 3.5mm headphone jack. A USB-A adapter is included in the box.

Software Updates
Security updates are released monthly. Major OS feature updates are released twice per year. Devices receive security updates for 5 years from the original purchase date.

Accessories
Official NovaBook sleeves and docking stations are sold separately. Third-party chargers must support USB Power Delivery at 65W or higher.

Repair Centers
Authorized repair centers exist in Berlin, Toronto, and Singapore. Walk-in repairs require an appointment booked through the support portal.
