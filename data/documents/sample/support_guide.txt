Acme Support Operations Guide
Internal reference for customer success agents

Order Status Codes
- PENDING: Payment authorized, not yet packed.
- PACKED: Order is in the warehouse packing queue.
- SHIPPED: Carrier scan exists; tracking number available.
- DELIVERED: Carrier confirms delivery.
- CANCELLED: Order cancelled before shipment.

Returns Workflow
Customers initiate returns from the Orders page. Once approved, a prepaid return label is emailed within 24 hours. After the warehouse receives the item, refunds are processed within 5 business days to the original payment method.

Live Chat Hours
Live chat support is available Monday through Friday, 09:00 to 18:00 UTC. Outside these hours, customers should open a ticket. Average first response time for tickets is under 12 hours on business days.

Escalation
Billing disputes above 500 USD must be escalated to the finance desk. Product safety complaints must be escalated immediately to the compliance team.

Knowledge Base Tips
When a customer asks about refunds, cite the 14-day refund window from the Customer Policy Handbook. When a customer asks about warranty coverage for accidental damage, clearly state that accidental damage is not covered.
